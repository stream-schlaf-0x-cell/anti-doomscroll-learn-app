# Product Specification: Atem (The AI-Native Antidote to Doomscrolling)

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy
Atem is an iPhone-first personal intellectual environment and reading feed designed to replace habitual, dopamine-driven social media scrolling.

### North Star Outcome
**More meaningful reading, discovery, understanding, and retention per minute of attention.**
- Not maximum session duration.
- Not endless swipes or algorithmic rage-bait.
- Not superficial flashcards or compulsory quizzes.

Reading is the primary, celebrated experience.

---

## 2. Target Profile (Starter Persona)
- **Role**: Philosophy/Ethics & German Teacher in Dresden, Germany (Saxony educational context).
- **Primary Device**: iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17/18.
- **Language**: German-first, original English sources accessible, toggleable.
- **Interests**:
  - Philosophy: Ethics, epistemology, philosophy of mind, existentialism.
  - Contemplative: Buddhism, mindfulness, meditation, psychology.
  - Tech & AI: Modern AI systems, Apple ecosystem, creative computation.
  - Education: Didactics, classroom experiments, pedagogical research.
  - Culture & Music: Electronic synthesis, ambient, Ableton, Logic Pro, contemporary history.
- **Ecosystem**: Craft, Raindrop.io, Apple Shortcuts, Gemini/OpenAI.

---

## 3. Content Taxonomy & Formats
Atem delivers dynamic, structured content across four core pillars:
1. **Current Affairs & News**: Verified provenance, German & European focus, tech/AI/science developments.
2. **Intellectual Discovery**: Depth without pretension; philosophical arguments, epistemology, cross-disciplinary connections.
3. **Professional Knowledge**: German didactics, ethics thought experiments, educational policy, classroom discussion seeds.
4. **Personal Context**: Followed topics, future Craft/Raindrop synchronization, personal project ties.

### Article Formats
- **Quick Insight** (~30–60s): Crisp summary of an emerging idea or breakthrough.
- **Briefing** (~1–3m): Synthesized multi-source update with context and key facts.
- **Deep Dive** (~4–8m): In-depth analytical or conceptual exploration.
- **Perspective** (~3–5m): Competing philosophical or ethical arguments compared fairly.
- **Connection** (~2–4m): Surprising intersection between disparate disciplines (e.g. Epistemology & Neural Network Interpretability).
- **Personal Update** (~1–2m): Development connected directly to an authorized followed topic.

---

## 4. Key User Journeys
1. **First-Run Onboarding**: Introduces the calm, intentional reading philosophy; allows selecting high-level topic weights and reading duration preferences; immediately drops into a rich feed without mandatory sign-up.
2. **Editorial Feed Discovery**: Visual variety with bespoke typographic treatments (hero cards, quote callouts, insight pills, reading time tags). Clear visual stopping point ("Daily Edition completed — continue into Discovery?").
3. **Focused Long-Form Reading**: Distraction-free typography, adjustable font size, light/dark themes, verified source attribution, reading time progress.
4. **Active Reflection & Deepening**: Optional post-read actions ("Explain Opposing View", "Connect to Ethics Teaching", "Dig Deeper", "Save to Library").
5. **Personal Library**: Saved articles, reading history, offline-ready local storage.
6. **Transparent Control**: "Why am I seeing this?" disclosure on every item; simple sliders for topic balance.
